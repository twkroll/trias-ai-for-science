## Verbindliche philosophische Boundary

Die stärkste zulässige Discussion-These lautet sinngemäß:

> Computational scientific success should be interpreted relative to the claim, referent, computational role, scope, and evidence relation actually established. Evidence for one form of success does not automatically establish the others; nevertheless, a system need not satisfy every relation in order to be scientifically useful for a specified purpose.

Nicht zulässig bleiben:

```text
- necessary trade-off among the three relations
- ideal center / optimal point in the triangle
- global ranking of role profiles
- mechanism as universal requirement for scientific success
- empirical claim that the vocabulary improves decisions or communication
```
